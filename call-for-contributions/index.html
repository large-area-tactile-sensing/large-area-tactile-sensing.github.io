Call for Contributions


Overview and Deadlines

We invite extended abstracts on topics related to large-area tactile sensing skins. We welcome contributions covering (but not limited to): Materials and Fabrication, Signal Processing and Data Management, Tactile Perception and Control, and Tactile Applications. This workshop aims to foster discussion and collaboration across robotics, soft materials, flexible electronics, and AI-driven perception.

The presentation format for accepted extended abstracts is a poster session and flash talk (TBD). For accepted submissions, at least one author must present on site at the workshop.

Important Dates

  • Submission Deadline: April 10, 2026 (11:59 PM AoE)
  • Notification of Acceptance: April 17, 2026
  • Workshop Date: June 1, 2026


Submission Instructions

Extended abstracts may contain previously published material and therefore do not require anonymization. Authors should list their names and affiliations and must cite any prior related work by the same authors.

These works can either represent preliminary results or report already published work. For the latter case, the original publication should be clearly mentioned with a variation of “this work is an extract from the paper [x]”.

Please submit your extended abstract through our Google Form submission portal:

Submit Your Abstract

You will need to provide:

  • PDF of your extended abstract (formatted according to guidelines below)
  • Author names and affiliations
  • Contact email for correspondence


Format Guidelines

  • Length: 2 pages maximum (including figures and tables), plus 1 additional page for references
  • Format: Standard IEEE two-column format, US letter size. Templates Here
  • Anonymization: NOT required (please include author names and affiliations)
  • Previously published work: Allowed. If presenting already published work, please clearly cite the original publication


Review Process

All submissions will undergo a peer review process by the workshop organizers and invited reviewers. Selection criteria include:

  • Relevance to workshop topics
  • Technical quality and originality
  • Clarity of presentation
  • Potential to stimulate discussion

Extended abstracts will not be published in formal proceedings, allowing authors to submit preliminary or already-published work.


Presentation Format

Accepted contributions will be presented as posters during dedicated poster sessions at the workshop. Authors are encouraged to prepare interactive demonstrations when possible.

Poster specifications:

  • Size: A0 portrait orientation (recommended)
  • Will be displayed throughout the workshop day
  • Authors expected to be present during designated poster sessions
